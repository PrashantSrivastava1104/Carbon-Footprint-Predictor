What this does

You enter a month of lifestyle data. An XGBoost model estimates your CO₂e in kilograms, labels it Low / Medium / High, and surfaces your biggest contributing factor.

Important caveat

The dataset is synthetically generated, not from real surveys. The high R² reflects the model re-learning the formula, not real-world accuracy. See the About page for full limitations.

Thresholds (India-calibrated)

  • Low — under 141 kg CO₂e / month
  • Medium — 141 to 209 kg
  • High — above 209 kg
